DevPocket Privacy Policy

Developer: TechHive Innovations

Last updated: September 8, 2026

DevPocket is a developer utility app by TechHive Innovations that helps users save snippets, favorite tools, and sync selected app preferences. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

DevPocket may collect and store the following information:

  • Account information from Google Sign-In, such as your Google account identifier and basic profile information provided through Firebase Authentication.
  • App data you create or choose to save, including code snippets, favorite tool IDs, theme preference, notification preference, and last tool behavior preference.
  • Basic usage and diagnostic information through Firebase Analytics, such as screen views and app interaction events.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide Google Sign-In and identify your account for cloud sync.
  • Sync your saved snippets and preferences across devices.
  • Improve app reliability, usability, and performance.
  • Maintain app functionality and troubleshoot issues.

Data Storage and Processing

DevPocket uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Analytics. Your synced app data is stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore under your authenticated user account. Some data may also be stored locally on your device for app functionality.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the app, including Google Firebase. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the app.

User Choices

You can use app controls to sign out of Google Sign-In, pause cloud sync, clear cached data, or clear user data stored in the app. You may also manage Google account permissions through your Google account settings.

Data Deletion

If you want your cloud-synced DevPocket data deleted, contact us at the email address below. Please include the Google account email associated with your DevPocket sign-in so we can identify the relevant account data.

Children's Privacy

DevPocket is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at support@orcafind.com.
